About

A founder who still ships the hard parts.

I am Muhammad Haris, in Islamabad. I lead product engineering at Zurox Tech and work as a full stack engineer at Evamp & Saanga, deputed to AXIAN Group.

Engineering with the operation in view

I work from the workflow outward: who can post, what a refund does to stock, which tenant a request belongs to, and what the person at the counter sees on a small screen. The products at Zurox Tech — restaurant POS, inventory and procurement, Marhaba Food House ordering, TenantManager, and General Setup — are that kind of software.

Before Zurox I spent August 2022 to May 2026 as a web application developer at Futuresol Technology Consultants, building ERP on ASP.NET Core, SQL Server, and Dapper, including multi-tenant administration. In 2022 I also taught as an instructor at Buraq Institute. I earned a BS in Computer Science from PMAS Arid Agriculture University, 2018–2022.

I value explicit boundaries, secure defaults, predictable failure handling, and code that stays readable as a product grows. When a system misbehaves I follow it through the application, the database, and the deployment until the cause is clear.
